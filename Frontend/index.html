History

The Liceu Nacional de São Tomé e Príncipe (LNSTP) is an educational institution in São Tomé and Príncipe, located in São Tomé, the country’s capital.


It is one of the oldest secondary schools in the nation. It currently has an estimate of 6,000 students.

The lyceum was opened on 6 October 1969 as a technical school, Escola Técnica Silva e Cunha. The building was designed by architect Mário de Oliveira, who was also responsible for the 1962 urbanization plan for São Tomé.


It became a liceu after independence. For 36 years, until 2011, it was the only high school in the country.
